Now the $SAVEM movement rises, a chaotic coalition of nostalgic weirdos determined to give these fallen heroes a second chance at virality. Every token purchased adds another bed to the Retirement Home. Every share delays a meme's deletion from collective memory. This isn't investing—it's internet archaeology with financial consequences. The memes saved us from boredom. Now we save them from oblivion.
